DeepRead is a local web library for technical books you already have the right to read. It turns a print PDF or a folder of markdown notes into a sectioned, searchable edition: sidebar, previous/next, figures, tables, and math.

This repository ships the reader and the extractors. It does **not** ship copyrighted books. The sample volume you are reading is original documentation. Editions you extract stay on your machine in `content/catalog.local.json` and are gitignored so they are not published by accident.

Buy or otherwise license a book, keep the PDF private, then extract it. That is the whole product.
